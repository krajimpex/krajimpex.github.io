How a diamond is grown

[ 01 ] The reactor

A chamber built to make carbon into diamond.

Laboratory-grown diamonds are grown in a sealed reactor by chemical vapour deposition, or CVD. Inside it, carbon is laid down atom by atom in exactly the crystal structure of a mined diamond.

Microwave plasma CVD · 2.45 GHz

[ 02 ] The seed

Every stone begins as a slice of diamond.

Thin, polished plates of diamond are set on a stage at the heart of the chamber. Their crystal lattice is the pattern every new atom will follow.

Seed: a polished single-crystal diamond plate

[ 03 ] The atmosphere

Hydrogen, with a breath of methane.

The air is pumped out and a little gas let in: mostly hydrogen, with a small share of methane, which carries the carbon.

Hydrogen with a few per cent methane · far below atmospheric pressure

[ 04 ] The plasma

Microwaves light a ball of plasma.

Microwave energy turns the gas into a glowing plasma thousands of degrees hot. It breaks the methane apart and sets its carbon free just above the seeds.

Plasma: several thousand °C · seed held at roughly 700–1,200 °C

[ 05 ] Growth

One atomic layer at a time.

Carbon settles on each seed and bonds into its lattice: about eleven million atomic layers for every millimetre. Growth is measured in weeks.

Typical rate: a few to tens of microns an hour

0 atomic layers
[ 06 ] The rough

A rough crystal, ready to be read.

What leaves the reactor is a rough diamond. It is examined and planned exactly as a mined rough would be, for the stone that returns the most light.

Some rough is treated after growth to refine colour; the report always says so

[ 07 ] Cut and polish

The same facets, the same hands.

It is cut and polished to the same standard as the natural room: the same proportions, the same angles, checked to a fraction of a degree.

Laser-sawn, shaped, then polished on a diamond-coated wheel (scaife)

[ 08 ] Declared

A diamond, and always declared as grown.

Chemically, physically and optically it is diamond. Every stone is independently graded and always declared as laboratory-grown.

Report number and a lab-grown mark, laser-inscribed on the girdle

Natural and grown

The same diamond. A different origin.

A laboratory-grown diamond is not a simulant. It is carbon in the diamond crystal, with the hardness, brilliance and fire that come with it.

What differs is where and how it formed, and the house never lets that go unsaid: natural and laboratory-grown diamonds are kept in separate rooms and declared on every document.
