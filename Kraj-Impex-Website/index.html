Cut for light

A diamond is judged by one thing: what it does with light. Every facet is placed to take light in, turn it, and send it back to the eye as brilliance, fire and scintillation.

A fraction of a degree in the angle of a facet decides whether a stone glows or falls flat. That is why the house looks at the cut before the carat, and shows only stones that return light fully.
