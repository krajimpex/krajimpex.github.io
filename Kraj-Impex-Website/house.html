The director

Kiritbhai Nathubhai Vaghani

Kiritbhai Nathubhai Vaghani is the director of Kraj Impex Diamonds. He directs K RAJ IMPEX LLP, the diamond firm incorporated in 2019 from which the house has grown.

His standard is simple: a stone is shown only if he would be proud to put the house’s name beside it, and it is always declared for exactly what it is. Under his direction the house keeps its circle small, answers every introduction personally, and treats each sale as the start of a long relationship.
