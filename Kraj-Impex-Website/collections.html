Shapes we hold

Each cut returns light in its own way.

Round brilliant

The most fire of any cut, and the shape against which all others are judged.

Oval

A brilliant drawn long, so the stone looks larger for its weight.

Emerald

Long step facets that show a stone's clarity like a hall of mirrors.

Pear

Half round, half marquise: a single point that leads the eye.

Cushion

Soft corners and broad facets that hold a warm, slow sparkle.

Marquise

Pointed at both ends; the longest-looking stone for its carat weight.

Shapes shown as drawings. Ask your advisor which stones are in the house today.
